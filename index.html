About

A student still figuring things out.

Not a resume. Just where I am right now.

I'm a student and a JEE aspirant with a genuine interest in how computers and software work. I spend a lot of my time experimenting—writing code, breaking things, fixing them, and occasionally building something that actually works. The process matters more to me than the result, and I'm okay with that.

I'm still working out what I want to specialize in. For now, I'm enjoying the process of learning and building—one project, one error, one fix at a time.

Approach

How I learn.

The method isn't glamorous, but it works.

I learn by building things slightly beyond what I currently understand. Not because it's efficient, but because that's where the actual learning happens. The moment I'm comfortable, I'm not really learning anything new.

When something breaks—and it always does—I look at the error, read the documentation, search for what I don't understand, experiment with different approaches, and try again. It's not the fastest way to learn. But it's the way that actually sticks.
