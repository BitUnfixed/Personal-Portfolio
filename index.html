01 / About

Still figuring it out.
That is the point.

I'm a student and JEE aspirant from Assam, India, with a genuine interest in software and technology. I'm still learning, experimenting with different corners of the field, and trying to understand what I actually want to spend more time with.

I enjoy the moment when something confusing starts making sense. I like taking things apart, building a rough version, and seeing where it breaks. I don't have everything figured out yet, and I think that is a useful place to be.

Outside the screen, I collect words and quotes, listen to music, read fiction, play games, and keep finding little stories, films, shows, and internet corners that linger longer than expected.

I also want to use AI in the things I build. Not to skip the learning, but to ask better questions, explore ideas faster, and make useful things with one of the most interesting tools of this era.
